About

R.A.S.T.E.R. is an interactive analog horror experience where every decision is recorded. The story reacts to what you do — and the tape never lies.

Every frame is generated in real-time. Every decision is permanent. The world doesn't wait for you to figure things out. Survival is earned, not guaranteed.

Why This Is Different

Real-Time Generation

Every frame is created by AI the moment you make a decision. No pre-rendered assets. No templates. The facility exists only as you explore it.

True Permadeath

When you die, it's over. No restart button. No checkpoints. Your entire playthrough is recorded to a VHS-style video file and preserved.

Unrestricted Actions

No multiple choice. Type what you actually want to do in natural language. The AI understands context and enforces consequences.
